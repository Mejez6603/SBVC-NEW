# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want SBVC to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone who uses or contributes to it — church operators, volunteers, developers and translators alike — regardless of age, background, experience level, gender identity, nationality, religion or denomination, or any other personal characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a good community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including to people who disagree with us
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the software in their services

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language
- Mocking another person's beliefs, language, faith tradition or skill level
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Using SBVC (including Live mode and chat) to harass others or to spread malware, scams or harmful content
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a community setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and comments — and when someone represents the project in public. It also applies to behavior inside SBVC's Live mode chat when it is used in connection with the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate or harmful.

To report a problem, open a **private** report through the repository's *Security → Report a vulnerability* page or contact the maintainer, [@Mejez6603](https://github.com/Mejez6603), through GitHub. All reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in order of seriousness:

1. **Correction** – a private or public note explaining what was inappropriate. A warning is enough for a first, minor incident.
2. **Warning** – a formal warning with consequences if the behavior continues.
3. **Temporary ban** – no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** – for a pattern of violations or serious harm.
